Privacy & Security

Privacy built into pricing intelligence.

Pricenaux is designed with a privacy-first architecture that separates customer-specific business data from the product identifiers used to support market intelligence.

Customer-specific information remains scoped to that customer and is used only to support that customer’s pricing and intelligence workflows.

Explore Security & Trust
  • Account-level isolation keeps customer-specific information separated.
  • Identifier-based market intelligence uses product identifiers such as ASINs and SKUs without exposing unrelated customer data.
  • Secure access controls limit sensitive information to authorized users.
  • Explainable AI and human control keep pricing decisions reviewable rather than blindly automated.
