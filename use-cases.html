Pricenaux supports different pricing environments without forcing every merchant into the same workflow. Start with the use case that best reflects how you sell, how much product data you have, and what kind of pricing decision you need to make.

Shopify Brands

Use your own commercial evidence to make better pricing decisions.

Shopify brands can combine SKU-level order history, product performance, demand sensitivity, market context, and margin constraints to understand how pricing decisions may affect the economics of each product.

  • Analyze historical order and product performance data
  • Understand demand sensitivity to price changes
  • Evaluate revenue, units, and margin together
  • Support new products through Launch Pricing
  • Bring market intelligence into SKU-level decisions
Explore Shopify Pricing Intelligence

Amazon Sellers

Use the pricing evidence that fits each Amazon product.

Amazon sellers can use Pricenaux to evaluate their own product performance, interpret marketplace pressure around an ASIN, or establish a market-informed starting price for new and low-data products.

  • Use order history, product performance, and demand sensitivity for product-based recommendations
  • Track selected competitor price movement and marketplace conditions
  • Monitor observed seller and Buy Box activity where available
  • Evaluate whether to move, partially respond, hold, wait, or reposition
  • Use Launch Pricing for new or low-data Amazon products
  • Protect margin instead of blindly matching lower prices

Many merchants sell the same or related products through both Shopify and Amazon. Pricenaux helps teams consider Shopify product performance alongside Amazon marketplace context so pricing decisions are not treated as isolated channel events.
