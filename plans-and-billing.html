What Each Capability Means

Pricing intelligence features built for margin protection and strategic control.

Pricenaux plans include more than basic pricing tools. Each capability is designed to help Amazon sellers, Shopify brands, and e-commerce teams make pricing decisions with stronger context, clearer controls, and less reliance on reactive price cuts.

Capability What it helps you do Why it matters Related Pricenaux capability
AI Price Recommendations Generate product-level pricing recommendations using sales behavior, product signals, demand sensitivity, and pricing performance data. Helps merchants move beyond guesswork and make more confident pricing decisions based on how each SKU or ASIN is actually performing. Dynamic Pricing
Competitive Price Intelligence Monitor selected competitors, compare pricing pressure, and understand how your product is positioned in the market. Supports competitor-aware pricing without forcing blind competitor matching or unnecessary price wars. Market Intelligence
Launch Pricing Price new or early-stage products when transaction history is limited or unavailable, using structured pricing methods and market-aware assumptions. Gives merchants a smarter starting point for new products instead of relying only on instinct, cost-plus pricing, or competitor copying. Market Intelligence
Profitability Guardrails Apply pricing controls, safe ranges, and profitability-aware recommendations before making price changes. Helps preserve profitability, reduce unnecessary discounting, and support long-term pricing discipline. Dynamic Pricing & Market Intelligence
Custom Pricing Rules Define pricing rules around brand position, margin targets, risk tolerance, competitor pressure, and approval workflows. Lets advanced merchants and agencies build pricing workflows that reflect their own strategy instead of using one-size-fits-all automation. Custom Intelligence
Competitor Monitoring Track selected competitor products over time and understand pricing gaps, market movement, and category-level pressure. Helps merchants respond strategically to competitor movement instead of copying prices automatically. Market Intelligence
Two Dynamic Pricing Modes Evaluate the same SKU or ASIN through two pricing perspectives: Market-Driven Dynamic Pricing and Product Performance-Driven Dynamic Pricing. Helps merchants compare what the market is signaling against how the product itself is performing, so pricing decisions are more strategic, explainable, and margin-aware.
