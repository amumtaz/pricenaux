How it works

How Pricenaux turns market signals into pricing decisions

The Pricenaux workflow blends continuous data collection, structured analysis, and AI interpretation to produce pricing decisions that are explainable, defensible, and aligned with your business goals. Each step is designed to reduce guesswork and increase decision confidence.

Collect → Analyze → Recommend → Automate → Learn

Collect

Pricenaux collects product-level ASIN and SKU data, selected competitor prices, marketplace signals, and product performance indicators. This gives each product a market-aware foundation instead of relying on isolated price snapshots.

Over time, continuous tracking helps reveal pricing pressure, competitor movement, and category-level shifts that merchants may miss manually.

Analyze

The intelligence engine evaluates competitor behavior, demand sensitivity, product signals, market structure, and pricing leverage. AI interpretation is combined with structured mathematical models to identify risk and opportunity.

This analysis helps Pricenaux move beyond simple repricing and toward strategic, transparent pricing guidance.

Recommend & Learn

Pricenaux generates a recommended price, safe range, confidence score, and clear reasoning behind the recommendation. Teams can review recommendations manually or use defined controls for automated execution.

As new market signals and product performance data appear, the platform continues learning from the product’s environment and strengthens future recommendations.

Pricenaux is not a repricer — it is a pricing intelligence platform that interprets market signals instead of reacting to competitor prices.
