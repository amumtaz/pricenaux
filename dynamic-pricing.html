Why Two Modes?

One SKU. Two pricing perspectives. Stronger pricing decisions.

A product’s best price is shaped by both the market around it and the product’s own performance history. Market-Driven Dynamic Pricing helps merchants understand competitor pressure, market structure, and brand positioning, while Product Performance-Driven Dynamic Pricing helps merchants understand demand response, revenue behavior, and margin opportunity.

Pricenaux is not powered by AI alone. Each pricing perspective is strengthened by structured mathematical pricing models that evaluate signals, compare scenarios, estimate opportunity, and support transparent recommendations.

Each mode can produce a recommended pricing range and an optimum recommendation, helping merchants compare risk, opportunity, and confidence before taking action.

AI interprets the market

AI helps Pricenaux read market signals, competitive intensity, product context, and pricing patterns so merchants can understand what is happening around each ASIN or SKU.

Mathematical models structure the decision

Mathematical models help evaluate margin impact, demand sensitivity, safe price ranges, confidence levels, and the trade-offs between revenue, profit, and positioning.

The result is not a black-box price. It is a clearer pricing decision supported by intelligence, equations, and business strategy.

Example Pricing Insight

Same SKU. Two pricing perspectives. One strategic interpretation.

For the same SKU or ASIN, Pricenaux can compare what the market is signaling against how the product itself is performing. Each mode can produce a recommended pricing range, an optimum recommended price, and a clear explanation behind the result.

Market-Driven Recommendation

Recommended range: $40.99–$42.49

Optimum recommendation: $41.99

Competitive intensity is moderate, but the product has enough positioning strength to avoid unnecessary discounting.

Performance-Driven Recommendation

Recommended range: $38.99–$40.25

Optimum recommendation: $39.49

Historical sales behavior suggests demand sensitivity increases above $40, with stronger profit stability near $39.49.

Strategic Interpretation

The market supports a higher price, but product performance suggests caution. A safe test range may sit between $39.49 and $41.99 depending on the merchant’s objective.

If the goal is margin and positioning, the market-driven recommendation may lead. If the goal is demand stability, the performance-driven recommendation may be safer.

Pricenaux does not just recommend a price. It explains the range, the optimum recommendation, and the trade-off behind the decision.
