Pricing Education

What is dynamic pricing software?

Dynamic pricing software helps e-commerce businesses adjust prices based on changing market conditions, competitor activity, product performance, and business goals. For Shopify brands and Amazon sellers, the right pricing system should do more than react to competitor prices — it should help preserve profitability, understand trade-offs, and make pricing decisions more structured.

01

It uses product and market signals

Evaluate product performance, sales history, competitor movement, margin goals, and market pressure before recommending a pricing direction.

02

It supports better pricing decisions

Use pricing insight to understand when to hold price, adjust price, or review a product’s market position.

03

It should preserve profitability

A strong pricing system does not simply match the lowest price. It helps merchants stay competitive while protecting profitability.

Practical Example

Shopify product facing competitive intensity

A Shopify merchant sells a premium desk lamp for $89. A competitor drops a similar product to $79. A basic repricer may push the merchant to match the lower price immediately. Pricenaux helps the merchant review product performance, margin sensitivity, rival movement, and pricing objective before deciding whether to hold price, adjust slightly, or protect premium positioning.
